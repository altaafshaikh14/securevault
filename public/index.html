How does Breach Scan work?
  • Checks your logins against a list of passwords leaked online (Have I Been Pwned).
  • Your passwords never leave this device — only the first 5 characters of a scrambled code are sent.
  • Needs internet and runs only when you tap the button. Everything else works offline.

1. Biometrics & Unlock PIN

Disabled

Biometric unlock uses your device's platform authenticator (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or Android biometrics) with the WebAuthn PRF extension to derive a hardware-bound key. Browsers or devices without PRF support cannot enroll.

The Unlock PIN is mixed into the master-password key derivation, so the vault cannot be decrypted without it. Changing it requires your current master password.

Biometrics: Not Enrolled

2. Website Auto-Fill

A web page cannot read or fill forms on other websites, so real auto-fill and auto-capture require a browser extension. Until then, use Copy & Open on any login card: it copies the password (auto-cleared after 30 seconds) and opens the site.

3. Cross-Device Encrypted Sync

Copy your encrypted Sync Payload to move this vault to another device. Pasting a payload replaces the vault on this device (the current one is kept as a previous-version backup).

4. Password Manager Migration

Import CSV exports from Chrome, Firefox, Bitwarden, 1Password or SecureVault (columns are detected from the header row). CSV exports are unencrypted — delete them after use.

How is Health calculated?
  • Only website logins are checked (cards, notes and passkeys are skipped).
  • Starts at 100%, then loses 10 per weak, 12 per reused and 20 per breached password.
  • Weak: too short or simple, easy to guess.
  • Reused: same password on more than one login.
  • Breached: found in leaks by Breach Scan (Security Center).
  • Checked on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
